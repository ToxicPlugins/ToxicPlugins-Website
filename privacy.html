How this early public preview works.

No online registrations or payments. This preview does not accept customer accounts, payments, or support submissions.

Browser favorites

The Store's saved-plugin feature uses local storage in your own browser. It stays on your device and is not sent to ToxicPlugins by this showcase.

Hosting and fonts

The hosting provider may process technical access data, such as network addresses and request logs, to deliver the website. The interface also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which makes requests to Google's servers.

Report builder

The Support page creates text in your browser only. It does not automatically send a report or collect your email. Do not put private keys or personal information into logs you share elsewhere.

Before full launch

This overview will need updating before accounts, support submissions, payments, or analytics are enabled.
