01 / THE FEATURES

BUILT WITH
PURPOSE.

Thought-out mechanics. Survival-first gameplay. Everything here is based on the stable build — not hypothetical features.

01

CRAFTABLE FROM DAY ONE

The custom crafting recipe is available to regular players, with no OP permissions required.

↗
02

FOUR UPGRADE LEVELS

Progress through four levels. The generator retains its level across server restarts and when it is picked up and placed again.

↗
03

AUTOMATIC DIAMONDS

At level four, the generator produces one diamond every five minutes when configured with supported storage.

↗
04

INVENTORY INTEGRATION

Attach a chest or barrel on top while sneaking and right-clicking to collect generated diamonds.

↗
05

OWNER PROTECTION

Other players cannot simply pick up your generator, preserving ownership in multiplayer.

↗
02 / HOW IT WORKS

FROM IDEA
TO IN-GAME.

01 /

CRAFT

Unlock and craft the generator using its custom recipe.

02 /

PLACE & UPGRADE

Place the block, right-click to open the upgrade interface and progress to level four.

03 /

COLLECT

Sneak + right-click to attach a chest or barrel, then collect the produced diamonds.
