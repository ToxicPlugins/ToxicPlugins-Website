PRIVACY MATTERS.

Privacy overview for the ToxicPlugins website and beta customer accounts.

Customer accounts (beta). We are preparing an account service for My Library. No premium purchases or payment collection are enabled.

Customer login data

If customer registration is activated, our authentication provider Supabase processes your email address, login authentication data and account identifier. We do not store your plaintext password in website files. Account credentials are handled by Supabase Auth.

Your plugin library

Our database stores which plugins you have claimed or are entitled to access, associated with your account identifier. Access is restricted to your own records by database permissions. A free claim does not constitute a payment.

Browser storage

The site stores sign-in session information in your browser so you remain signed in. The Store also uses browser local storage for optional favorites.

Hosting, providers and security

Netlify hosts this website and may process technical request logs. Supabase provides login and account database services. Google Fonts and a JavaScript CDN may receive standard browser requests when pages load.

No live checkout

ToxicSkullFactory is listed at €1.99, but online payment processing and premium JAR delivery are not enabled. The Support page currently builds bug report text locally without submitting it automatically.

Account privacy requests

Account deletion, access requests, retention periods, operator contact details and the formal privacy notice must be finalized by the website operator before opening registration to the public. Please do not use the beta for personal data beyond a test account until those details are published.
